Tunedrift privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

What Tunedrift collects

Nothing. Tunedrift has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive any data about you or about how you use the app.

What stays on your device

Your music library, playlists, favorites, listening history, settings and downloaded songs are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes its data.

Online services the app contacts

When you search, browse or download music online, the app sends your search words directly from your device to public music services (such as Deezer's public catalog, YouTube and lyrics services) to show results, covers and lyrics. These requests are not sent to the developer. Each service handles them under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Music and audio files: to read and play the songs on your device.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show the player and keep music playing with the screen off.
  • Internet: for online search, covers, lyrics and downloads that you start.

Children

Tunedrift is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/captentv1/tunedrift.
